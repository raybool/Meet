FAQ

Questions about the concept

How is this different from searching inside TikTok or Instagram?

The concept is cross-platform semantic search. Instead of a broad keyword on one platform, you describe the exact situation and receive a smaller set of content ranked for that intent.

Does MotivFeed host creators' videos?

The intended model is a discovery layer that sends users to original creator content on supported platforms, rather than becoming a replacement hosting network.

Can I search for workout, study or productivity motivation?

Yes. Those are core use cases being tested, alongside career, confidence, discipline and self-improvement.

Is the app free?

Pricing has not been decided. The research intentionally tests whether people prefer free-only access or would pay for consistently better matching and personalization.
